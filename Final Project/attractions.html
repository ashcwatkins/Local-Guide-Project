North Carolina State Fair

Every year, Raleigh hosts the North Carolina State Fair. They have food vendors, carnival rides, live entertainment, and agricultural displays. It is a fun place for both residents and tourists, to visit whether it is their first year or tenth!





Hours of Operation:

Mid-October to Late-October

9 a.m. - 11 p.m.

4285 Trinity Rd, Raleigh, NC 27607

Pullen Park

Pullen Park is a historic park called in Raleigh, North Carolina. It has playgrounds, a tiny train, and a carousel! The park provides an enjoyable and safe place for outdoor activities, and is a well-liked location for families and kids.





Hours of Operation:

Everyday

7 a.m. - 9 p.m.

Ashe Ave Raleigh, NC 27606

Juniper Level Botanic Garden

In Raleigh, North Carolina, there is a stunning botanical garden called the Juniper Level Garden. It provides tourists a tranquil setting and a range of plant types, both native and foreign. Families, wildlife lovers, and photographers are sure to visit this garden!





Hours of Operation:

Monday-Friday

8 a.m. - 5 p.m.

Sauls Road, Raleigh, NC 27603
